Who We Are

Ecozonmax Blindbrand (OPC) Private Limited ek aisi company hai jo modern packaging aur prakriti (nature) ke beech ke antar ko khatam kar rahi hai. Hum Vill: Sukahara se shuru hokar pure Bihar aur Bharat mein sustainable solutions pahunchane ke liye pratibadh hain.

Hamara vishwas hai ki behtar business wahi hai jo dharti ka khayal rakhe. Hum premium quality ke paper bags, jute products aur customized eco-friendly boxes taiyar karte hain jo plastic ka sabse behtar vikalp hain.
